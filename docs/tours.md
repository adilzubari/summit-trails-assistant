# Summit Trails — Tour Catalog

Summit Trails is a boutique guided-hiking company based in Banff, Alberta. Small groups,
certified guides, all gear included. Below are our current tours.

## 1. Sunrise Summit (Half-Day)
- **Duration:** 5 hours (departs 5:00 AM)
- **Difficulty:** Moderate
- **Group size:** Max 8
- **Price:** CAD $129 per person
- **Includes:** Certified guide, headlamps, trekking poles, hot breakfast at the summit, photos.
- **Best for:** First-timers and photographers. Kids 12+.

## 2. Glacier Ridge Full-Day
- **Duration:** 9 hours
- **Difficulty:** Challenging
- **Group size:** Max 6
- **Price:** CAD $249 per person
- **Includes:** Guide, crampons, helmet, packed lunch, safety briefing.
- **Best for:** Experienced hikers. 16+ only. Good fitness required.

## 3. Family Meadow Walk
- **Duration:** 3 hours
- **Difficulty:** Easy
- **Group size:** Max 12
- **Price:** CAD $69 per person (kids under 6 free)
- **Includes:** Guide, snacks, wildlife spotting guidebook.
- **Best for:** Families with young children.

## 4. Private Custom Expedition
- **Duration:** Flexible (half to multi-day)
- **Difficulty:** Your choice
- **Group size:** Private (1–10)
- **Price:** From CAD $600 per day (whole group)
- **Includes:** Dedicated guide, custom route planning, all gear.
- **Best for:** Corporate groups, special occasions, serious adventurers.

## Booking & Logistics
- Book online or by WhatsApp. A 30% deposit confirms your spot.
- Meeting point: Summit Trails base, 210 Bear Street, Banff.
- We provide boots on request (sizes 5–13) at no extra charge.
- Tours run rain or shine; only cancelled by us for lightning or extreme conditions.
