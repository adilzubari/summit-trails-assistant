# Summit Trails — Refund & Cancellation Policy

## Customer cancellations
- **7+ days before the tour:** full refund, minus a CAD $15 processing fee.
- **3–6 days before:** 50% refund.
- **Under 48 hours:** no refund, but you may reschedule once within 12 months at no charge.
- **No-shows:** no refund and no reschedule.

## Weather cancellations (by Summit Trails)
- If we cancel for safety (lightning, wildfire smoke, extreme conditions), you get a
  **full refund or a free reschedule** — your choice.
- Light rain or snow is not a cancellation; tours run rain or shine.

## Rescheduling
- Free reschedule if requested **3+ days** ahead. Under 3 days incurs a CAD $25 fee.
- Reschedules are subject to availability and must be used within 12 months.

## Deposits
- The 30% booking deposit is refundable under the same timeline as above.

## How to request
- Email hello@summittrails.example or message us on WhatsApp with your booking name and date.
- Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5–7 business days.
