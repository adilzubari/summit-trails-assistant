# Summit Trails — FAQ

**Do I need experience?**
Not for the Sunrise Summit, Family Meadow Walk, or a private easy route. Glacier Ridge is
challenging and 16+ only, with good fitness required.

**What should I bring?**
Water, weather-appropriate layers, and sunscreen. We provide poles, headlamps, safety gear,
and boots on request (sizes 5–13, free).

**What's the group size?**
Small by design: 6–12 depending on the tour. Private expeditions are just your group.

**Are kids allowed?**
Yes on the Family Meadow Walk (under 6 free) and Sunrise Summit (12+). Glacier Ridge is 16+.

**What happens if the weather is bad?**
Tours run rain or shine. If we cancel for safety, you get a full refund or free reschedule.

**How do I pay, and is my spot confirmed?**
A 30% deposit confirms your booking. Pay online or via WhatsApp. Balance is due on the day.

**Where do we meet?**
Summit Trails base, 210 Bear Street, Banff, Alberta. Sunrise tours depart 5:00 AM sharp.

**Can you do corporate or private groups?**
Yes — the Private Custom Expedition covers corporate offsites, celebrations, and custom routes,
from CAD $600/day for the whole group.

**Do you offer refunds?**
Yes, on a sliding scale by how far ahead you cancel. See our refund policy for details.
